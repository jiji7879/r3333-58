## 1. Independent minimum-28 semantics

`owner_semantics_audit.py` is stdlib-only and does not import campaign proof code. It reads the revised labels, statement, and pin rule, then independently:

1. checks 150 sorted unique labels, owner rank 147, and the exact 28 ranks/cover pairs;
2. checks the stated transformation from the old 29-pair list: remove three pairs and add two;
3. recomputes all ten local owner pins and the physical identities of the eight retained pins;
4. reads each of 29 colorings as 78 edge colors, checks every triangle and the nominated pentagon, checks all ten pins, and recomputes occurrences among all 147 earlier pairs;
5. checks 20 pairwise-disjoint singleton groups, six further pairwise-disjoint groups, and three pairwise constraints on a disjoint set of three ranks;
6. independently regenerates the complete unordered 13-vertex CNF, including site-word equivalences, occurrence definitions, and equal/unequal pair exclusions.

Run: `python3 owner_semantics_audit.py`.

Exact output is `evidence/semantic-stdout.json`; its key fields are:

```json
{"status":"PASS","earlier_pairs":147,"cover_pairs":28,"lower_bound":28,
 "geometric_witnesses":29,"private_deletion_witnesses":28,
 "dimacs_sha256":"ab78dd02501e7efbd3b411fa863bb202ad61f4034977963b8e588e7584463d6a",
 "dimacs_bytes":41960}
```

The lower bound is a hitting-set argument. Every cover needs 20 choices for the singleton constraints, six further choices for the six disjoint constraints, and at least two of three mutually disjoint ranks to meet all three pair constraints. The generated 28-pair UNSAT query uses only eight pins. Since its pin set is a subset of the ten used by the lower witnesses, the upper bound applies with ten pins and the lower bound applies with eight. Hence the exact minimum is 28 under either pin set.

The same 29 lower witnesses intersect the new cover in 28 distinct singleton hits, one for every new-list deletion. Separately, actual execution of `audit_owner.py --verify` accepted all 37 compact deletion witnesses, all 29 ten-pin alternative-deletion witnesses, and the 160 original-owner witness convention records. Output: `evidence/owner-deletions-stdout.txt` (0.10 s, empty stderr).

**Remaining premise:** the 147-pair universe here is precisely the supplied `campaign/cover-edits/13.json`. This direction did not replay the separate cover-edit derivation connecting that file to the original arm catalogue. The new 28-pair UNSAT proof does not rely on the old rule's cached status or old RUP certificate.
