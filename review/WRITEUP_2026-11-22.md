One swarm (`smwrmwgf7`, twelve members, deepseek-v4.1-flash at max reasoning) ran
on the wall profile of the `R_4(3) <= 58` cover. This is the account of that
session: ten accepted results, six record claims withdrawn or corrected, two
member-directory theorems moved into the kernel, and a great deal still open.

**Status unchanged where it matters: W2 is open, all 381 cases are OPEN, and
`R_4(3)` is still in `[51, 62]`. No case was closed and no bound moved.** Nothing
below should be read as progress toward a closure; it is progress in knowing
where a closure cannot come from.

## 1. The setting, in one page

A frozen cover reduces `R_4(3) <= 58` to 113,179 cases (79,264 CERTIFIED, 31,751
OPEN). The profile `(15,14,14,14)` holds 381 of the OPEN ones (308 `T---`, 73
`U---`). At a root `r`: `A = N_0(r)` has 15 points, the three arms `B, C, D` have
14 each, `X = B u C u D`, and W2 asks for a contradiction from a vertex of this
profile with `H(v) >= 15 = w(v)`.

The **uniform fully-sending branch** is the working hypothesis: every point of `A`
is 0-critical (`d_0(a) = 15`) and every outside vertex has `m(x) = |N_0(x) ∩ A| =
5`. Inside it, `d_0(a) = 15` forces a's fourteen colour-0 partners to sit in the
three arms, `s_B(a) + s_C(a) + s_D(a) = 14`, and mixture bounds each arm part by 5
— so **every arm part is 4 or 5**, which is the "ten 5s and five 4s" law.

## 3. What was withdrawn, and why

This is the part of the session I would defend hardest. Six recorded claims did not
survive checking. Each is corrected in place in `review/PROFILE_15141414_STATE.md`
and `review/WALL_15141414_SWARM.md`, not merely noted here.

### 3.1 `M >= 7` was never proved; `M >= 6` is

The record claimed `M = max_{u != v} |N_0(u) ∩ N_0(v)| >= 7` in the branch, by a
`C_x` counting chain plus a final "`M = 6` rigidity" step. The chain is:

    C_x = sum_z C(d(z),2) over the 41 sites of X \ {x},  sum d = 65,  d(z) <= 5,
    C_x <= 10(M-2),

and with the `d_0(x) - 5` sites that are 0-adjacent to `x` forced to `d(z) = 0`
(otherwise the 0-triangle `x, z, p`), the sharp minimum is `3 d_0(x) - 8` — 37 at
`d_0(x) = 15`, 40 at the largest admissible `d_0(x) = 16`. Both give `M >= 6`. To
reach 7 one needs `C_x >= 51`, i.e. `d_0(x) >= 20`, which the degree cap forbids.
**The final rigidity step was asserted and never derived.**

Withdrawn with it: `sum (n_{aa'} - 4)^2 >= 294` has no source (the exact identity
gives `sum n^2 >= 420` and nothing above), and with that the conclusion "the 42
blocks are not a `2-(15,5,4)` design" — the design case is arithmetically
consistent and is exactly the case where all 105 arm codegrees equal 5.

What survives and is used: `M >= 6`, which is what the spoke-table fact "the root
is never in a maximising pair" needs. Found by member m6; I re-derived it, and
corrected one step of m6's own account (it read the record's inequality as
unjustified when in fact it is the zeros-sharpened one).

### 3.2 `14+14` is SAT — but not the object the route needs

The record concluded "the two-arm instance is SAT, so `UNSAT at 14+14` can never
prove the branch; that route is dead." The witness behind it
(`smwrfgma5/m4/wit/a_14_14.wit`) has **258 monochromatic cross-arm triangles** and
column sums `[8, 9, 10]` — one position at 8, violating the exact law `s_B + s_C in
{9,10}` — and its words admit **no** cross-arm-triangle-free pair colouring at all.

So what is SAT is the pair level plus the arm module with coverage `<= 10`, which is
not the object heredity delivers. **The rung with `XT` is UNKNOWN, not SAT.** The
two-arm route is hard, not closed, and the record no longer says otherwise.

### 3.3 The stated reason for `|armFar| <= 4` was not in the tree

The bound is true and now certified (240 DRAT instances + a sharp witness), but the
justification recorded for it — "the K59 argument transfers because its only size
input is the exchange rule on 16-sets with a 15-core" — cites
`K58.exchange_sixteen` / `exchange_fifteen`, and `rg -n exchange lean/` is **empty**.
The bound is now established by a finite exhaustion (X is a 16-template minus two
points, 240 cases) with the classification as its only structural input.

### 3.4 The Hall inequality was "not violated" only because the test could not see it

`WALL` §27 reported: with `I = A` and 300 random 15-subsets `J`, checking subsets of
size at most four and the full set, "no violation occurs", so the inequality does
not eliminate the witness. The test was structurally blind: every word has exactly
five 0s, so `|N_0(S) ∩ A| >= 5 > |S| - 1` for `|S| <= 4`, and `|S| = 15 = J`
usually covers `A`. The violation lives at `5 <= |S| <= 14`.

Exhaustively, the margin has the closed form `min(alpha_0, 30) - 16` and the five
pair-level witnesses have `alpha(G_0) = 34, 41, 36, 30, 30` — **all five violate at
margin 14**. The inequality is nevertheless inert: it is *equivalent* to
`alpha(G_0) <= 16`, hence implied by the X-internal triangle family the pair level
omits.

### 3.5 "An all-extremal arm cannot fill 14 slots" was a non sequitur

The record argued: three copies of one word force its empty colour, so copies `<= 2`
per arm, and therefore no all-extremal arm fills 14 slots. Seven distinct extremal
words twice each is fourteen slots, and there are **2,244,026** all-extremal size-14
arms passing pairwise admissibility. The conclusion is true, but only with the
depth-3 condition: three copies of one extremal word already have all three
admissible lists equal to one singleton, and under that condition the count is
**0 for every arm colour**.

`K58.uniform_branch_zero_critical` / `K58.ten_outside` (the reciprocity chain the

## 6. My own errors, this session

* I first ran m12's 332 witnesses through my own graph6 decoder and reported **all
  332 invalid**. The bug was mine: I decoded the bits row-major instead of nauty's
  column order. The witnesses are valid; caught within the same turn.
* I derived an `M >= 8` bound from the `C_x` chain before noticing that the number
  of forced-zero sites is `d_0(x) - 5`, not `d_0(x)`; the correct ceiling is 6. It
  was caught before it reached the person or the record, but it is the same slip I
  withdrew in §3.1, made in the opposite direction.
