## Why the new cover is exhaustive, and its exact scope

The local formulas encode a complete 3-coloring of the nominated arm, prohibit monochromatic triangles, and fix the ordered pentagon. For each arm size and each possible second sorted word `z`, the 304 new certificates refute a sorted arm that avoids every retained pair. Duplicate labels are blocked on adjacent sorted sites, which is equivalent to blocking two occurrences anywhere under the sorting constraint. Every sorted complete arm consequently contains a retained pair.

For a physical row, the outside vertices are anonymous after the frozen signed units are applied. `geometry.py` was rerun on all 11,585 rows: the only incident signed pins on those vertices are the common root-color and marked-vertex conditions, and the marked-template maps were regenerated byte-for-byte. Sorting these vertices by spoke word is therefore a symmetry, not a new mathematical restriction. The physical map uses the exact nominated pentagon, arm palette, and first-tier parent; no formula was rewritten.

For a coloring, choose the lexicographically first retained pair occurring in its arm, nominate two vertices with those words, and sort the rest. In `pair_extension`, monotonically nested gates forbid earlier pairs when the previous gate is true. Ownership at rank `j` is the previous gate (if any) together with `-gate[j]`. This precisely selects the first occurring retained pair; it is not an arbitrary paired-color preference.

The first-tier parent is the ordinary `row_formula(row, 'first')`, with tables and total-critical constraints and without full-root or perfect-balance restrictions. A local RUP step remains RUP after the injective physical variable renaming and against a superset formula. Exact parent graph clauses, signed pins, sorting/ownership clauses, and physical cube are included in the regenerated queries. These certificates justify the **representative partition**, not refutation of its members.

## Joining edge: exact units, mandatory guard, and counts

For words `w,z`, the immediate triangle rule permits precisely

```text
between(w,z) = {0,1,2} minus {w[i] : w[i] = z[i]}.
```

If a shared pentagon spoke already has color `c` at both outside vertices, joining-color `c` would form a monochromatic triangle. This part is owner-independent.

I checked the complete new-cover classification: 2,126 normalized pair/color classes = **2,091 SAT models + 35 refutations**. Every SAT model was independently checked as a complete arm with the first selected pair, sorted remaining sites, pinned words, and asserted joining color. Global row multiplicities reproduce:

- 97,283 affected representatives;
- 92,533 newly reduced to one joining color;
- 97,363 excluded color children (from 2,326,245 triangle-surviving children to 2,228,882).

All 35 new exclusions have local RUP evidence and a regenerated exact full-first-tier representative RUP proof. There are 35 distinct full queries on 30 distinct parent hashes. **Every one of the 35 receipts has `scope = round50`; none has raw owner-free scope.** Their derivation uses the prior-pair-absence/current-pair ownership guard. The corresponding negative physical literal is sound only with that exact row, arm palette, pair, and guard. Dropping the guard or transporting the unit or learned consequences to a differently guarded/plain job is not justified.

The safe physical conversion is the exact `arm['palette'][c]`, not an assumption that normalized and physical color numbers coincide. Edge exclusions can be supplied as per-call assumptions for the matching guarded query. If they are installed as permanent clauses, learned-clause export requires separate assumption/scope discipline; this audit does not establish such transport.

No further support exclusion is certified here. All 5,930 signed marked-neighborhood/new-pair projections have checked colorings. These projections rule out a local-support refutation; they do not prove full K58 satisfiability or provide a new unit for the 3-second solver.

## Consequences for the bounded campaign

- The new 1,101,974-job queue is a certified exhaustive naming reduction, **not 1,101,974 solved or unsolved verdicts**.
- Every retained pair and every surviving joining color has local support, so the local cover/edge tables alone cannot close a new-cover representative. A full-parent refutation must use omitted vertices or other global first-tier constraints.
- The first 50,000 new-cover runs and their edge-enabled counterparts each report zero closures at the 128-conflict cap. That is neither a 3-second measurement nor evidence about all 1,101,974 jobs. I derive no solver preference from it.
- A coordinator may use a local sorted exclusion as progress only after regenerating its exact first-parent/guard/cube lift and strictly checking the resulting proof, as in the retained example. A local label or a proof for another parent hash is insufficient.
- Root status remains **OPEN = not certified closed**, not SAT. No whole-row or Ramsey-number conclusion follows.
