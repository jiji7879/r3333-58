# Independent-model check

Four accepted directions of `smwziqvco` used `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`
under the same 75-minute, 100-step limits. They did not supply an independent
family check, but did give independent implementations and reconstructions.
The coordinator individually re-ran their central claims before acceptance.

- m1/d1 now retracts the one-of-eight normalization objection: its defect was
  a generator pin to the group root colour. Fresh explicit vertex/group colour
  relabellings pass. Accepted only for finite template normalization and
  exact local implications, not global embeddings.
- m2/d2 (direction C) regenerated all 14 first-tier parents, compared complete
  clause sets, checked original units and guard semantics in both directions,
  and reran bounded controls. Accepted as measurements, not wall closure.
- m4/d3 (direction D) recomputed the 13 historical controls after its first
  guard-key comparison bug was rejected. Accepted with its corrected scope.
- m3/d4 audited 14 claims against exact artifacts and reimplemented
  addition-only strict RUP. Accepted after a fresh RUP/native/model rerun.
  Its own §4 explicitly refuses to extend the exact K15 CNF to every K58
  neighbourhood without the normalization supplied by d1.

There were two initial reports rejected for missing case analysis and a bad
control-diagnostic field; no mathematical direction was accepted on the
strength of the original README. The corrected reports all retain the OPEN,
local-model and no-Ramsey limits.
