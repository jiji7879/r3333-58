# Duggan method assessment

**Status: PROVED as a bounded applicability assessment, not as a Ramsey theorem.**
All eight independent directions were accepted after reading the artifacts and
rerunning their controls. The useful next steps are (1) finish one exact-parent
Lean/DIMACS semantic assembly using the native encoding-extension lemmas and
(2) test conditional-boundary Sinz counters on one ordinary wall parent. The
other tested routes do not justify production compute. No wall row, degree
profile or published Ramsey bound changed.

## Established results

* **Critical exact generalisation is impossible:** the $K_{15}/K_{16}$ critical
  links are alternative-closed, so every Gauthier–Brown exact gray-edge
  generalisation is a singleton. Checked 1,800 witnesses, rejecting controls and
  pure-kernel representative theorems (#7).
* **Boundary-only conditional counters:** leaving the Sinz recurrence
  unconditional and guarding only overflow/bound clauses preserves exactly the
  projected constraint $g\Rightarrow\sum x_i\ge k$ and strengthens propagation.
  All 168 actual gadgets passed matched SAT/BCP controls. One small actual A/B
  was slightly slower, so no performance claim is made (#11).
* **No preferred-full-vertex transfer at $K_{58}$:** three disjoint checked
  Clebsch graphs provide $e(3,16;m)\le120$ on every relevant order, while
  $29d-d^2\ge180$. Each colour's full-vertex deficit is therefore at least
  3,480, and the count $58-\phi$ guarantees no full vertex. The paper's literal
  generic identity needs an $e(H_1)$ term, but its actual $p=3$ uses are safe
  (#14).
* **Historical static lex was vacuous:** every auxiliary true satisfies the old
  comparator. The corrected stabilizer-lex encoding passed semantic/DRAT
  controls but made its pilot substantially slower. Dynamic SMS remains open
  and needs explicit frozen-stabilizer witnesses (#18).
* **Soft-constraint model finding has no demonstrated $K_{51}$ benefit:** one
  corrupted $K_{50}$ was repaired and checked, but the informative $K_{51}$ unit
  and Toeplitz pilots were UNKNOWN. Small/minimised cores are the useful next
  experiment, not larger undirected budgets (#21).
* **Native encoding-extension lemmas now exist:** native at-least, guarded
  counters and guarded P4/C5 support selectors have old-variable extension
  proofs with clean standard axioms and exact artifact controls. `AtLeast.lean`
  was only auxiliary-sign-renamed, not native PySAT clause order. Full-parent
  assembly has not been done (#24).
* **One-colour maximal closure is sound but not a solver win:** it gives
  $\sum_{u\in N_c(v)}d_c(u)\ge n-1$, and degree at least four at $n=51/58$ for
  that one fully normalised colour only. Full and pinned MCF did not beat the
  sampled wall baseline (#28). The coordinator's separate configurable-edge
  maximal normal form preserves the non-host suffix, passed 807,936 small
  controls and was neutral at one million conflicts on one wall row.
* **Duggan's pinned 2024 MCTS implementation is incompatible:** the prior
  array/action layout does not cover the four-colour K58 actions. Current
  generic MCTS produced a strict five-leaf OPEN wall frontier and lost to greedy
  end-to-end on the solvable control (306.456 versus 0.720 seconds of cubing and
  284,528 versus 226,881 conflicts). Eleven second-checker replays passed (#31).

## Checks performed

All eight member reproductions were rerun independently. They include all 1,800
saturation obligations; guarded-counter clause/projection/BCP and actual gadget
checks; 1,098 small graphs and the full-vertex artifact identities and Lean
arithmetic; symmetry semantic orbit checks, strict microproof and all eight
timing calls; soft-constraint exact enumeration, verified $K_{50}$ model and
one strict conditional branch DRAT with wrong-parent rejection; the contiguous
2,536-clause real encoder suffix and the three Lean axiom reports; 542,952
small MCF colourings, 117,720 incidences, actual $K_{50}/K_{40}$ colorings and
matched wall caps; all MCTS cover audits and eleven second-checker replays.

The coordinator's configurable maximal MCF CNF was regenerated byte-identically.
Its exhaustive control covers all 3,156 good 4-colourings of $K_4$, all 64 fixed
edge subsets and every target colour. The million-conflict original/augmented
runs both stayed OPEN. The integrated `lean/build.sh` passes with the three new
modules registered in `lean/K58/Check.lean`.

## Open gaps and skipped checks

* No exact full-parent Lean/DIMACS assembly or support classification binding
  was attempted. The semantic bridge is the next real certificate task.
* No unrestricted SMS/CAS integration was built or benchmarked. No general claim
  about all MCTS policies follows from these one-row/one-control experiments.
* No $K_{51}$ colouring was found. Every UNKNOWN/OPEN status remains undecided.
  No local search result is evidence of UNSAT.
* The MCF augmentation is an equisatisfiability normal form, not an original-
  parent DRAT implication. It needs its separate semantic proof before any
  closure could be admitted. Its neutral pilot does not justify production use.
* The final synthesis has only the coordinator's `self` review, as the gate
  states. Component arguments have distinct `model`, `compute` and (where
  applicable) `lean` checks. No separate independent-model gap review of the
  synthesis itself was launched; that check is explicitly not claimed.
