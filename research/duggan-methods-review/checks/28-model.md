Reviewed m8's one-colour closure and star-degree argument. Safe recolouring adds only c edges, removes only edges of other colours and requires no global c-path, so it preserves all colour classes' triangle freedom and terminates with MCF. The same does not justify joint maximality of several colours or preservation of a fixed star. Every nonneighbour of v needs a distinct edge from N_c(v) to some w, giving sum(d(w)-1)>=n-1-d(v) and sum d(w)>=n-1. With the degree cap 16 this gives degree at least four only for the fully normalised colour. Full/pinned MCF claims are not being applied to the separate suffix-invariant configurable-F normal form.
