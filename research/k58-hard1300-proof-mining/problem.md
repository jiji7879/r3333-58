No ordinary wall re-benchmark, additional toolkit installation, cohort production-ledger change, or Ultramath. Artifacts live only in `.scratch/hard1300-proof-mining/` and this research folder. OPEN is not SAT or UNSAT; only a complete warning-visible native proof replay establishes the case. Clause implication proofs must not be mistaken for complete row or cohort proofs.

Done means a fresh checked hard-case refutation, comparison of the actual core/backward structure between the cases, and either (a) a nontrivial physical clause/schema with explicit role/pin/guard/aux-definition witnesses and fresh native checks on the other exact hard parent, or (b) a precisely stated obstruction to that generalisation. Already certified D/rank1300 clauses, square transfers, paired-arm ternaries and elementary palette-resolved triangles are not new answers. No case-local learned clause is presented as a general theorem without its logical premises and destination proof.
