# Coordinator reading of the whole four-package audit

## Claim and scope

The aim is to determine the mathematical and certificate content of four supplied archives and the distinction between solver-search measurements and amortized benefit. The audit result is scoped to the finite listed parents and local rules; it does not decide the unresolved wall or the Ramsey bound.

## Checks against overreach

- The spoke rule is a complete local two-color/list 2-SAT implication argument. Exhaustion covers all tested graph/list cases and the six K4 obstructions are exactly square+matching. Its native checks derive consequences on actual original wall parents. No guarded K15 theorem or full wall formula is logically subsumed merely because the local schema is broader. All 16 capped trials are OPEN and their total CPU is higher.
- Mined direct clauses are consequences of their exact original parent. The two additions in D are tested for preservation on physical variables and separately from any parent refutation. Every semantic square binding has its own fresh RUP steps. The fifth-color and premise-deletion counterexamples prevent treating the ternary escape rule as arbitrary-color general. A different square clause is not allowed to inherit D's timing figure.
- A projection is a subset of a parent, not an auxiliary augmentation. Projection UNSAT implies parent UNSAT, but projection SAT does not imply parent SAT. Five complete raw proof streams have deletion-only normalization, followed by warning-fatal native RUP and independent LRAT checks on their respective full parents. The two local models falsify many clauses in their full parent, so are not presented as K58 colorings.
- The requested comparison sources have exact hash/parent bindings and full binary-LRAT terminal-empty/RUP-only checks. Trimmed source versus unavailable raw solver proof is explicitly distinguished. The 5,250 and 5,245 cores belong to distinct valid searches. Canonical clause overlap is 5,094, whereas exact literal-line match is 4,359; these two counts answer different comparisons.
- Corrected metadata was re-read: rank 1294, 1295 and 1297 are TTT-, and rank 1298 alone among them is TTU-. The initial projection report with the incorrect kind labels was not accepted.
- Search benefits are not discovery/certificate benefits. D saves about 8.91% conflicts and 7.99% proof-logging CPU over two seeds of one historical row, but the partial mining stages cost substantially more than the saved solve time. P13000 has higher conflicts, CPU and proof size than original A0. Growing a proof or executing an OPEN capped trial does not establish a completed wall row or amortized gain.

## Finding

No remaining gap in the scoped result. Residual limitations are only those explicitly retained: local clauses rather than global completion, finite selected parents rather than the unresolved cohort, extra provenance caveats where native checker inputs were trimmed, and no full cold end-to-end speed measurement.
