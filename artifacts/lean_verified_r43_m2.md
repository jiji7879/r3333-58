## 2. Reusable certificate checker (for any K_51 claim)

    python3 lean/gen_cert.py CERT.col > Check.lean && lean Check.lean        # seconds
    python3 lean/gen_cert.py --kernel CERT.col > Check.lean && lean Check.lean   # axiom-free, ~4 min

`CERT.col` is the format required by `tools/verify_coloring.py`. The generated file proves
symmetry, zero diagonal and absence of monochromatic triangles, and concludes
`∃ c, Symm c ∧ (∀ i, c i i = 0) ∧ TriFree c` (hence `R_4(3) > n-1`). For n=51, k=4 that is
20825 triangles checked by the kernel. So any claimed improvement can be certified by a
proof assistant, independently of my Python and of `tools/verify_coloring.py`.

Negative control: `lean/bad.col` is the valid K_50 colouring with edge (0,1) recoloured to
colour 0. `tools/verify_coloring.py` reports 4 monochromatic triangles, and Lean's
`native_decide` reports "the proposition TriFree c is false" — the checker is not vacuous.

## 3. Machine-checked structural lemmas (`lean/Struct.lean`, axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`)

* `R43.R33`   : every 2-colouring of the edges of K_6 has a monochromatic triangle (R(3,3) ≤ 6).
* `R43.R3_le_17` : every 3-colouring of the edges of K_17 has a monochromatic triangle
  (R(3,3,3) ≤ 17, by the neighbourhood pigeonhole 16 > 3·5).
* `R43.four_deg_le_sixteen` : in a 4-colouring of K_n with no monochromatic triangle every
  vertex has **at most 16 edges of any one colour** — the hypothesis that d4's profile split
  and every other member use; it is now proved, not assumed.
* `R43.sum_colour_degrees` : the four colour-degrees at a vertex sum to n-1.
* `R43.R4_le_66` : every 4-colouring of K_66 has a monochromatic triangle (pigeonhole
  corollary: n-1 ≤ 4·16).

Consequence (counted from the two lemmas, not in Lean): the admissible colour-degree
profiles at a vertex of a mono-triangle-free 4-colouring of K_51 are the 4-tuples summing
to 50 with all entries ≤ 16: **47 of them up to order, 680 ordered** (d4's "~50 profiles"),
and the minimum of Σ_c C(d_c,2) at a vertex is 288, attained only at (12,12,13,13).

## 4. Correction to the symmetry lemma quoted in d2

d2 says: order-3 automorphisms are impossible, "so Aut has order coprime to 3 and is
intransitive; hence a nontrivial Aut contains a fixed-point-free order-17 element with 3
orbits of 17". The first part is right: any nontrivial order-3 permutation of 51 points has
an orbit {x,gx,g²x}, and g cyclically permutes the three edges of that triangle, forcing a
monochromatic triangle. The inference is not: |Aut| coprime to 3 does **not** give 17 | |Aut|.
Nothing rules out an involution (25 two-orbits + 1 fixed point), or elements of order 5
(10 five-orbits + 1 fixed point), 7 (7 seven-orbits + 2 fixed), 11 (4 eleven-orbits + 7
fixed), 13 (3 thirteen-orbits + 12 fixed), or order 17 with fixed points. In each of those
cases the only g-invariant 3-sets consist of a 2-orbit (whose two equal-coloured edges
leave the third free) or of fixed points, so no monochromatic triangle is forced. Hence
d2's quotient decides the vertex-transitive/order-17-semiregular class (all groups of order
51 are cyclic: n_3 | 17 and n_3 ≡ 1 mod 3 gives n_3 = 1, n_17 | 3 and n_17 ≡ 1 mod 17 gives
n_17 = 1), but it is not "all symmetric colourings".

## 5. Base cases, both bounds (so the degree bound is sharp)

`lean/Cert_K5_2col.lean` and `lean/Cert_K16_3col.lean` are generated by the same script from
`lean/k5_2col.col` (the 5-cycle) and `lean/k16_3col.col` (a mono-triangle-free 3-colouring
of K_16, found by a 1-second SAT call and then checked twice). Together with `Struct.lean`
this gives, machine-checked in Lean:

    R(3,3)   = 6    [ Struct.R33 (<=)  and Cert_K5_2col   (>=) ]
    R(3,3,3) = 17   [ Struct.R3_le_17 (<=) and Cert_K16_3col (>=) ]
    51 <= R(3,3,3,3) <= 66  [ Cert.lean (>=51), Struct.R4_le_66 (<=) ]

So `four_deg_le_sixteen` ("a vertex has at most 16 edges of a colour in K_51") is exactly
sharp: 16 = R(3,3,3) - 1. Everything the swarm's case splits assume about degrees is now
proved rather than assumed.

## 6. Independent count of the K_51 near-miss in artifacts/

`artifacts/k51_relax_m3_1defect_NOT_a_valid_colouring.col` was recounted with an algorithm
disjoint from `tools/verify_coloring.py` (sum over vertices and colours of pairs of
same-coloured neighbours divided by 3): it has **exactly one** monochromatic triangle,
(48,49,50) in colour 1. Hence the minimum number of monochromatic triangles over all
4-colourings of K_51 is 0 or 1, and the whole question is whether that last triangle can be
removed.

## 7. The Ramsey-local relaxation does not decide R_4(3) (verified counterexample)

d9's conditions are (a) every 6-set uses ≥3 colours (⟺ ω(G_a ∪ G_b) ≤ 5 for all colour
pairs) and (b) every 17-set uses all 4 colours (⟺ α(G_c) ≤ 16). Exact bitset max-clique
computations (`.ultramath/smwiiyj7u/m2/relax_check.py`, exact branch and bound, not a
heuristic) on `artifacts/k51_relax_m3_1defect_NOT_a_valid_colouring.col` give

    max over colour pairs of omega(G_a u G_b) = 5     -> (a) holds
    max over colours of alpha(G_c)              = 16    -> (b) holds, bound attained

yet that colouring has exactly one monochromatic triangle. So the two local conditions admit
K_51 colourings and cannot decide R_4(3); every successful argument needs more. Sanity
checks of the script: the valid K_50 certificates also satisfy (a),(b) (as the machine-checked
lemmas force), the K_16 3-colouring gives α = 5 = R(3,3) − 1, the C_5 2-colouring ω = 5.
